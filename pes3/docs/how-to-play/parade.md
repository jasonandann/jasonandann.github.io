# 🎈 Monster Parade

**The goal:** Get a whole line of silly monsters ready for the big parade!

## How to play
1. A new monster arrives. **Give it a name** or tap **Skip** for a made-up name.
2. The monster needs things to get ready. For each one, tap **Go** and answer a question:
   - an **extra** (wings, tail or fur)
   - something to **wear**
   - something to **hold**
   - a **ride** (like a band wagon, balloon or unicycle)
3. **Right answer?** Three choices pop up. **Tap your favorite** to give it to the monster!
4. **Not quite?** "Let's try another question!" and you get a new one.
5. When every monster is ready, the **parade** starts and your monsters march across the screen! 🎉

## Saving
Tap **Save** at the end to keep your parade. Find it later in **My parades**.

## Tips
- Tap **⚙️ Options** to pick spelling, math or both, and how many monsters (3 to 7).
- Pick things that go together, or the silliest things you can find!
