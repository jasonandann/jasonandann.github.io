# 🔢 Number Cascade

**The goal:** Find the right answer on a board full of numbers.

## How to play
1. A math question shows up, like `8 × 3 = ?`
2. Below it is a board of 16 number tiles.
3. **Tap the tile** with the answer.

## What happens next
- **Right tile?** A ripple of color spreads across the whole board! Then comes the next question.
- **Different tile?** It gives a little shake. Try another!
- There are 10 questions in a game.

## Tips
- Work out the answer first, then hunt for it.
- Tap the ⚙️ in the corner to change the kind of math.
