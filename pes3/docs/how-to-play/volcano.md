# 🌋 Word Volcano

**The goal:** Tap the letter rocks in order to spell the word and make the volcano erupt!

## How to play
1. **Listen** to the word. Tap 🔊 to hear it again.
2. Letter rocks are scattered on the volcano. A few are extras that are not in your word.
3. **Tap the rocks in spelling order.** Each right rock moves up into the boxes.
4. Watch the lava rise with every right letter!

## What happens next
- **Right rock?** It joins your word and the lava gets higher.
- **Different rock?** It wiggles. Try another!
- If you get stuck, the next rock starts to glow.
- Spell the whole word and the volcano erupts with lava, smoke and confetti! 🎉
- Tap **Next ➜** for a new word.
