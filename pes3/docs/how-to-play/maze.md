# 🚪 Number Maze

**The goal:** Choose the right door in each room to get through the maze.

## How to play
1. A math question shows up, like `9 − 4 = ?`
2. You see **three doors**. Each door has a number on it.
3. **Tap the door** with the right answer.

## What happens next
- **Right door?** It swings open and you walk into the next room.
- **Different door?** It stays shut. Try another door!
- There are 8 rooms in the maze.

## Tips
- Solve the problem in your head first.
- Tap the ⚙️ in the corner to change the kind of math.
