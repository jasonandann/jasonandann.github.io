# 🫧 Number Bubbles

**The goal:** Pop the bubble that has the right answer.

## How to play
1. A math question shows up, like `6 + 7 = ?`
2. Bubbles with numbers float around.
3. **Tap the bubble** with the answer.

## What happens next
- **Right bubble?** All the bubbles pop! 🎉 Then you get the next question.
- **Different bubble?** It pops by itself. Try another one!
- There are 10 questions in a game.

## Tips
- Solve the problem in your head first, then look for the number.
- Tap the ⚙️ in the corner to pick the kind of math: adding, times tables or dividing.
