# ✏️ Fill the Blanks

**The goal:** Some letters are missing. Find them and finish the word!

## How to play
1. **Listen** to the word. Tap 🔊 to hear it again.
2. The word shows up with empty boxes where letters are missing.
3. The glowing box is the one you are working on. (You can tap a different empty box if you want.)
4. **Tap the letter** on the keyboard that goes in the box.

## What happens next
- **Right letter?** It pops into the box and you move on to the next blank.
- **Different letter?** The word wiggles. Try another one!
- Stuck on one? After a few tries the game tells you the letter, so you can keep going.
- When all the blanks are filled, tap **Next ➜**.

## Tips
- Say the word slowly and listen for the missing sound.
