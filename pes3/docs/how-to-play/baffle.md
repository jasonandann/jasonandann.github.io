# 🤯 Baffle

**The goal:** Find words hidden in a grid of letters by sliding across letters that touch.

## How to play
1. You see a 5×5 grid of letter tiles. **Your spelling words are hiding in there!**
2. **Slide your finger** across tiles that touch each other (up, down, sideways, or corner to corner). The letters you pick build a word at the top.
3. Let go, or tap **✔ Check**, to try the word.
4. The word boxes show how many words to find. Words you find get filled in.

## What happens next
- **Found a spelling word?** It gets checked off. ⭐
- **Found a different real word** with 3 or more letters? That is a **bonus word**! 🫙
- Words you already found are fine. Nothing is lost.

## Need help?
- Tap **💡 Hint**. The next letter blinks and you will hear the word.
- You can use every tile only once in each word.

## Tips
- Look for the first letter of a word, then see which letters touch it.
