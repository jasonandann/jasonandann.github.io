# 👂 Listen & Type

**The goal:** Hear a word, then type it. It is just like a real spelling test!

## How to play
1. **Listen.** The word is said, and so is a sentence. Tap 🔊 to hear it again.
2. **Type** the word with the keyboard. Use ⌫ to erase a letter.
3. Tap **Check ✓**.

## What happens next
- **Right?** Hooray! Tap **Next ➜**.
- **Not quite?** The box clears. Listen again and try once more.
- After two tries, the game shows you the word. Look closely and type it.

## Tips
- Say the word slowly and write down each sound you hear.
