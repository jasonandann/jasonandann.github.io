# 🃏 Memory Match

**The goal:** Find all the matching pairs of words.

## How to play
1. You see a bunch of ⭐ cards, all face down.
2. **Tap a card** to flip it. You will see a word and hear it.
3. **Tap a second card** to flip it.
4. Do the two words match?

## What happens next
- **Match!** The pair stays face up.
- **No match?** The cards flip back over after a moment. Try to remember where the words were!
- Find every pair to win. Then tap **Play again** or **Home**.

## Tips
- Say each word out loud when you flip it.
- Remember where you saw each word. That is the whole trick!
