# 🛡️ Battle Buddies

**The goal:** Answer questions to fill the love meter with 💗 hearts.

## Getting started
Pick **how many players** (1 or 2), your **buddy** (an animal friend like 🦄 or 🐉), and a **theme**:
- **Love** – fill your heart meter! You can play **Race** or **Together**.
- **Battle** – send sparkles ✨ to pop your buddy's hearts.

Tap **⚙️ Options** to choose spelling, math or both, the level, how many hearts, and how tricky the computer is.

## How to play
1. Tap to start your turn. A question appears.
2. **Answer it.** Use the keyboard for spelling or the number pad for math.
3. **Right answer?** A 💗 floats into your meter (or a sparkle ✨ flies out in battle)!
4. **Not quite?** You get another try. Nothing bad happens, you just don't get a heart this time.
5. Then it is the other player's turn. If you are playing the computer, it takes its turn by itself.

## How you win
- **Race:** First to fill the meter wins.
- **Together:** Everyone works as a team to fill one big meter, and everybody wins together! 🎉

## Tips
- 1 player? You play against a robot buddy.
- Play **Together** if you want a calm, friendly game.
