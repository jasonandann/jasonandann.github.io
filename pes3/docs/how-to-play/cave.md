# 💎 Crystal Cave

> This game is hidden at first. A grown-up can turn it on in the grown-ups panel.

**The goal:** Explore the cave with your team, answer questions to earn cards, and scare away silly bad guys. Collect the most stars ⭐!

## Getting started
Pick who is playing (you, friends, or computer heroes), choose a hero, and tap **Start!**. Tap **⚙️ More options** for the cave size.

## On your turn
Pick one:
- **Explore** – tap a glowing tile to turn it over.
- **Face a bad guy** – use your cards to scare it away.
- **Buy a gem** – gems glow when you have enough cards.
- **Pass** – only if there is nothing to explore.

## Getting cards
When you find an ability tile, you answer a question to **earn a card**. Math earns power cards, spelling earns wisdom cards, and magic or treasure can be either one.

## Scaring a bad guy
You need the right cards. Don't have enough?
- **Ask for help.** Teammates may give you a card.
- **Be brave** and answer more questions to fill the gap.
- **Back away.** You lose a heart. If you run out of hearts you sit out one turn, then come back with a heart.

## Winning
When every tile is open, there are a few last turns. Then everyone counts their stars. In a team game, you win together!

## Tips
- If you share a device, pass it when it is someone else's turn.
- The game saves, so you can come back to it later.
