# 👁️ Spot Match

**The goal:** Find the one picture that is on both cards.

## How to play
1. You see two cards. Each card has lots of little pictures.
2. **Exactly one picture is on both cards.**
3. **Tap that picture.**

## What happens next
- **Found it?** Great spotting! You get a new pair of cards.
- **Not that one?** The game says "Look again!" Keep looking.
- There are a few rounds in a game.

## Tips
- Pick one picture on the first card, then hunt for it on the second.
- It might be big or small, and turned a different way.
