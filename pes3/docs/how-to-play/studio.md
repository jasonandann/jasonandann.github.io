# 🎨 Code Studio

**The goal:** Make your own little show! Pick sprites, then tell them how to move, talk and make sounds.

## How to play
1. You have a **stage** with a sprite (an emoji character) on it.
2. **Drag the sprite** to where you want it to start. You can also pick a different picture and background.
3. Build a script with colorful blocks:
   - 🔵 **Move:** ➡️ ⬅️ ⬆️ ⬇️, turn, go 🏠 home
   - 🩷 **Looks:** 💬 say something, 🎈 grow, 🤏 shrink, 🙈 hide, 👀 show
   - 🟢 **Sound:** 🎵 play a note, 💥 pop
   - 🟠 **Control:** ⏱️ wait, 🔁 repeat, ♾️ forever
   - 🔴 ⏹️ stop everything
4. Pick when the script starts:
   - 🏁 starts when you press **▶**
   - 👆 starts when you **tap the sprite**
5. Press **▶** and watch the show!

## Tips
- Add more sprites. Each one gets its own script, and they all run at the same time!
- Use ⏱️ wait to make sprites take turns talking.
- Your project saves by itself. You can come back later!
