# 🧟 Zombie Trail

**The goal:** Race along the trail to the finish. Roll the die and answer questions to move ahead!

## Before you start
Choose how many **players** and what kind of **questions** (spelling, math or both). Tap **⚙️ More options** for the number of zombies, jump sizes and more.

## How to play
1. **Roll the die** 🎲.
2. A question shows up. **Answer it.**
3. **Right answer?** Move ahead the number you rolled.
4. **Not quite?** You get a second chance. If you still miss, you move back that many squares.

## Special squares
- 🚀 **Rockets** are shortcuts. They zoom you forward!
- 🧟 **Zombies** send you back. Watch out and dodge them!

The trail winds back and forth. First one to the end wins!

## Tips
- With friends, take turns and cheer each other on.
- A shortcut only helps if you land on it.
