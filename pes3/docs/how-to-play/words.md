# 📖 See the Words

**The goal:** Get to know your spelling words before you play the other games.

## How to play
1. Look at the list. Every card is one of your spelling words.
2. **Tap a word** to hear it said out loud.
3. Some words also have a sentence. You will hear that too!
4. Tap as many times as you like. Nothing can go wrong here.

## Tips
- Say each word along with the voice.
- Look at the letters while you listen.
- Try this game first, then play **Word Jumble** or **Fill the Blanks**.
