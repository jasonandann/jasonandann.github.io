# 👀 Look, Cover, Write, Check

**The goal:** Study a word, hide it, then spell it from memory. Many teachers use this trick!

## How to play
1. **Look.** The word is shown. Look at it carefully. Tap 🔊 to hear it.
2. Tap **Cover it ✋**. The word disappears!
3. **Write.** Type the word from memory with the keyboard. The ⌫ key erases the last letter.
4. Tap **Check ✓**.
5. **Check.** The game puts your word next to the real one so you can compare the letters.

## What happens next
- **Matched?** Great job! Tap **Next ➜**.
- **Not quite?** Look at which letters are different. Then tap **Look again 👀** and try one more time.

## Tips
- Picture the word in your head after you cover it.
- Notice the tricky part of the word while you look.
