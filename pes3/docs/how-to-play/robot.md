# 🤖 Robot Course

**The goal:** Plan a path, press go, and help the robot reach the 🏁 flag!

## Your first time
The game asks for your robot's name. Type one, or tap **Skip**.

## How to play
1. Pick a course from **🗺️ Courses**.
2. Look at the board. Where is the robot? Where is the flag? Walls and cones block the way, and 💎 gems are fun to collect.
3. **Plan first!** Press the arrow keys on the robot's head to add steps: forward, back, turn left, turn right. Each step shows up as a colored light.
4. Press the orange **▶** key and watch the robot follow your plan.
5. Want to go one step at a time? Use the **Step** button.

## What happens next
- **Reached the flag?** 🎉 Tap **Next course ▶**.
- **Bumped into something?** "Let's fix the plan!" The robot's ghost shows where it stopped and little arrows show where it went. Change your steps and try again.
- Tap a step to **move it** or **🗑️ remove it**. The ✕ key takes off the last step. You can also drag steps around.

## Build my own
Tap **🧱 Build my own** to make a course. Use the tools to add walls, cones, gems, the robot and the flag. Then tap **🤖 Code it!** to try it.

## Tips
- Walk through the path with your finger first.
- Count the squares before you add steps.
