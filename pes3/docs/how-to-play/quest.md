# 🗺️ Study Quest

**The goal:** Go on a story adventure! Answer questions along the trail to finish your mission.

## Pick a story
You can choose one of these:
- 🥚 **Dragon Egg Quest** – ride a pony to bring a dragon egg home to Dragon Mountain.
- ⛵ **Island Voyage** – sail across the islands to bring a baby sea turtle home.
- 🏮 **Moonlight Lantern** – walk the night trail to relight the village lantern.
- 🌱 **Seed Ark** – gather rare seeds to regrow the Great Garden.
- 🚀 **Space Rescue** – fly past the planets to rescue a little alien.
- 🐎 **Pony Express** – ride the mail out West from Missouri to California.
- 📖 **Lost Library Pages** – find the pages of a magic storybook.
- ⏳ **Time Machine** – zoom through history to find the missing gears.

Tap ⚙️ **Options** to pick spelling, math or both, and the level.

## How to play
1. Each step on the trail uses up a bit of food.
2. At each stop, something happens. It might be a **question**, a **shop**, a **river crossing**, a **rest**, or a surprise **event**.
3. **Answer the questions** to earn what you need to keep going.
4. Reach the end to finish your story! You may find something special to keep.

If you stop in the middle, you can **Continue** later.

## Tips
- Read the story. The clues help you decide what to do.
- No hurry. It is an adventure, not a race.
