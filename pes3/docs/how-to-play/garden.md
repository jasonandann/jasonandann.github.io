# 🌻 My Garden

**The goal:** Watch your garden grow!

## How it works
- You don't play a game here. You just **look at what you have grown**.
- **Every time you finish a question** in any game, you earn a little something for your garden.
- Seeds sprout into **flowers**. The more you play, the bigger the garden gets.
- Your garden only grows. Nothing ever goes away.

## Tips
- Play lots of games, then come back to see what changed.
- Some special adventures give you extra plants to keep. Look for them above the garden!
