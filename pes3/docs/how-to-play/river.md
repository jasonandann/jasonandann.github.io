# 🐸 Lily Pad River

**The goal:** Help the frog hop across the river on the pads with the right answer.

## Before you start
Pick **Spelling**, **Math** or **Both**, and a math level.

## How to play
1. A question shows up. For spelling, listen and pick the pad that is **spelled right**. For math, find the answer.
2. Lily pads float down the river in two lanes.
3. **Tap the pad** with the right answer to make the frog hop.

## What happens next
- **Right pad?** Hop! On to the next question. There are 10 in a game.
- **Different pad?** That pad sinks. Try another one.
- If you miss twice, the right answer starts to glow to help you.

## Tips
- For spelling, read each pad slowly. Some are tricky!
- Don't worry about the pads moving. Take your time.
