# 🔎 Word Search

**The goal:** Find all of your spelling words hidden in the letter grid.

## How to play
1. Look at the word list. These are the words to find.
2. **Drag your finger** across the letters of a word. Words can go in any direction: across, down, up, backwards or on a diagonal.
3. Or **tap the first letter, then tap the last letter**.
4. Let go and the game checks your word.

## What happens next
- **Found it?** The word lights up and gets crossed off the list.
- **Not a word?** The letters fade away. Try again!
- Find every word to finish the puzzle.

## Tips
- Look for the first letter of a word, then check the letters around it.
- Don't worry about being perfect. A little wobble is okay!
