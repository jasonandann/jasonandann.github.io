# 🌧️ Spell Catcher

**The goal:** Catch falling letters in the right order to spell the word.

## How to play
1. **Listen** to the word. Tap 🔊 to hear it again.
2. Letters float down like rain. Some letters are not in your word. They are just tricky extras!
3. **Tap the first letter** of the word. Then the second, and so on.
4. Caught letters fill up the boxes at the top.

## What happens next
- **Right letter?** It pops into the next box.
- **Different letter?** It gives a little shake. Keep looking!
- Missed letters never disappear. They loop back to the top, so take your time.
- If you get stuck, the next letter starts to glow to help you.
- Finish the word and tap **Next ➜**.

## Tips
- Don't rush! Nothing runs out.
