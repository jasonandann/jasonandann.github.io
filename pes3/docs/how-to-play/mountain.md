# ⛰️ Number Mountain

**The goal:** Climb all the way to the top of the mountain by answering questions.

## How to play
1. A math question shows up, like `12 + 15 = ?`
2. Pick an answer from the buttons.

## What happens next
- **Right answer?** Your climber takes a step up the mountain! 🧗
- **Different answer?** That button turns off. Try another!
- 10 right answers get you to the flag 🚩 on the peak.

## Tips
- Take your time. The mountain is not going anywhere!
- Tap the ⚙️ in the corner to change the kind of math.
