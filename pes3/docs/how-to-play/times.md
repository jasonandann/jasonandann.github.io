# 🧮 Times Table Explorer

**The goal:** Learn the times tables by looking, practicing and spotting patterns.

You see a big grid of numbers from 1 × 1 up to 12 × 12. There are three ways to use it. Tap the buttons at the top to switch.

## 🔍 Explore
**Tap any square.** The game tells you what it is, like `4 × 6 = 24`. Look around and see what you find!

## 🎯 Practice
1. **Pick a number** from 2 to 12.
2. The squares for that times table turn into **?**.
3. Tap a **?** and pick the right answer from the choices.
4. Right answers fill in the square. Can you fill them all?

Pick a different answer if you miss. You can try again!

## ✨ Patterns
1. **Pick a number.**
2. All the answers in that times table light up.
3. Look at the glowing squares. Do you see a pattern, like a stripe or a zig-zag? ✨

## Tips
- Practice a small times table first, like 2 or 5.
