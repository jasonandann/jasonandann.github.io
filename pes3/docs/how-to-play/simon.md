# 🎵 Simon Says

**The goal:** Copy the pattern of colors and sounds. 

## How to play
1. Tap **Start**.
2. **Watch.** The colors light up and play notes, one after another.
3. When it says **"Your turn!"**, tap the same colors in the same order.
4. Each time you get it, the pattern gets one step longer.

## What happens next
- **Matched it?** Here comes a longer one!
- **Mixed one up?** No problem. You watch the same pattern again and try again.
- Get all the way to the end for an "Amazing memory!"

## Tips
- Say the colors out loud: "red, blue, green..."
- Listen to the notes too. They have a tune!
