# ❓ Guess What

**The goal:** Find the computer's secret word by asking questions about how words **sound**.

## Getting started
You see a board of your spelling words. The computer hides one of them. Pick **Just me** or **Take turns**.

## Just me
1. **Tap a question**, like "Does it start with a "b" sound?"
2. The game tells you **Yes** or **No**.
3. Now put away the words that don't fit. **Tap a word** to hear it and lay it down. (Tap again to stand it back up.)
4. Keep asking until only one or two words are left.
5. **Tap the word** you think is the secret one!

Guessed a word that isn't it? That card just goes down, and you keep playing.

## Take turns
1. Pick **your own secret word**. The computer will try to guess it!
2. The computer asks you a question about your word.
3. Say your word out loud to yourself, listen for the sound, and tap **Yes** or **No**.
4. Then it is your turn to ask!

## Tips
- Listen to the **sounds** in the word, not the letters.
- A good question gets rid of lots of words at once.
