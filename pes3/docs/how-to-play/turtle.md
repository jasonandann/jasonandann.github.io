# 🐢 Turtle Draw

**The goal:** Write a little program that makes a turtle draw a picture.

## How to play
1. Pick a mode at the top:
   - **🎯 Copy a shape** – draw over a shape you pick.
   - **🖍️ Free draw** – draw anything you want!
   - **🖼️ My drawings** – see your saved pictures.
2. The turtle starts in the middle, facing up.
3. **Tap blocks** to build your program:
   - ⬆ forward, ⬇ back
   - ↻ ↺ turn
   - ✏️ pencil up or down (up = moving without drawing)
   - 🎨 pick a color
   - 🔁 **repeat** – do some steps again and again
4. Press **▶** to watch the turtle draw.

## Using Repeat 🔁
Tap 🔁 to make a box. Tap blocks to put them inside the box, then tap **✅ Close the box**. A square is just *repeat 4 times: forward, turn*!

## What happens next
- **Copy a shape:** When your drawing covers the shape, you get a ⭐ and a **Next shape ▶** button. If it is not quite right: "Try changing a step!"
- **Free draw:** Tap **💾 Save** to keep your drawing.
- Tap a block to change its number, move it, or remove it.

## Tips
- 🐇 Fast and 🐢 Slow change how fast the turtle moves.
- Spot a pattern that repeats? That is a job for 🔁!
