# 🧩 Puzzle Pieces

**The goal:** Uncover a hidden picture one piece at a time.

## How to play
1. A picture is covered up by puzzle pieces.
2. **Tap a piece.** A math question pops up.
3. **Pick the right answer** and that piece goes away.

## What happens next
- **Right answer?** The piece disappears and part of the picture shows!
- **Different answer?** Try another one.
- Uncover every piece to see the whole picture!

## Tips
- Tap the ⚙️ in the corner to change the kind of math.
