# 🔀 Word Jumble

**The goal:** Put mixed-up letters in the right order to spell the word you hear.

## How to play
1. **Listen** to the word. Tap 🔊 to hear it again.
2. The letters are all mixed up at the bottom.
3. **Tap a letter** to send it to the next empty box.
4. Filled the wrong box? **Tap it** and the letter hops back down.
5. When every box is full, the game checks your word.

## What happens next
- **Right?** Hooray! Tap **Next ➜**.
- **Not quite?** The letters shake and mix again. Try once more!
- After a few tries, the game shows you the word so you can see how it is spelled.

## Tips
- Listen for the first sound and find that letter first.
- Say the word slowly in your head.
